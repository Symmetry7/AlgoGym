  - question: A fixed-size window of k elements moves one step right. How many elements change?

    answer: 0

  - question: When does a variable-size window shrink?
    options: ["Every step", "When the window breaks the condition", "Only at the end", "Never"]
    answer: 1
    why: Move the left edge only while the window is invalid, then keep growing from the right.
  - question: Each element enters and leaves the window at most once. What is the total time?
    options: ["O(n²)", "O(n log n)", "O(n)", "O(k)"]
    answer: 2
    why: Both edges only move forward, so together they take at most 2n steps.

## The idea

{/* 2–3 sentences. What problem shape this solves and the one move that makes it fast. */}

Many array problems ask about a contiguous stretch: the longest, the shortest, or one with a given sum. Instead of checking every stretch from scratch, keep one window and slide its edges.
