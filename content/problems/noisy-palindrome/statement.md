A puzzle app accepts a line as a palindrome if it reads the same forwards and backwards after you ignore case and every character that isn't a letter or a digit.

Return `true` if `text` is such a palindrome, and `false` otherwise. A line with no letters or digits counts as a palindrome.

## Constraints

- `1 ≤ text.length ≤ 10^5`
- `text` contains letters, digits, and the characters `. , ! ? - _ ' : ;` (no spaces).
