A hospital rotates its duty roster every week. One rotation moves every entry one place to the right, and the last entry wraps around to the front.

Given the roster `roster` and the number of rotations `k`, return the roster after `k` rotations.

## Constraints

- `1 ≤ roster.length ≤ 10^5`
- `−10^9 ≤ roster[i] ≤ 10^9`
- `0 ≤ k ≤ 10^9`
