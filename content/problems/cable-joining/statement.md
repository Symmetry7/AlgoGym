An electrician has several cables and needs one long cable. Joining two cables of lengths `x` and `y` metres produces one cable of `x + y` metres and costs `x + y` rupees.

Given the lengths `cables`, return the smallest total cost to join all of them into one cable. One cable needs no joins and costs `0`.

## Constraints

- `1 ≤ cables.length ≤ 10^5`
- `1 ≤ cables[i] ≤ 10^4`
