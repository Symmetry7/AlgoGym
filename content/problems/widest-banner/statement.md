For a festival, a rectangular banner will hang flat against a row of shops. Shop `i` is 1 metre wide and `heights[i]` metres tall. The banner must cover whole shops next to each other and can't rise above the shortest shop it covers.

Given `heights`, return the largest possible banner area in square metres.

## Constraints

- `1 ≤ heights.length ≤ 10^5`
- `0 ≤ heights[i] ≤ 10^9`
