A ration shop must hand out its bags in the order they were packed. Each day the counter hands out bags one after another, but it stops for the day when the next bag would push the day's total weight above the counter's capacity. That bag waits for the next day.

Given the bag weights `bags` (in kilograms, in packing order) and the number of days `days`, return the smallest whole-number capacity that lets the shop hand out every bag within `days` days.

## Constraints

- `1 ≤ days ≤ bags.length ≤ 5 × 10^4`
- `1 ≤ bags[i] ≤ 500`
